WHO OBSTETRIC DANGER SIGNS DIRECTORY

Severe Headache with Visual Disturbance

CRITICAL

Throbbing frontal headache unresponsive to standard hydration/rest, flashing lights, blurred vision, or seeing spots.

⚠️ Clinical Concern: Severe Preeclampsia / Impending Eclampsia

Sudden Swelling of Face or Hands

URGENT

Rapid-onset edema around the eyes, tight rings on fingers, or sudden weight gain greater than 2 kg in 48 hours.

⚠️ Clinical Concern: Systemic Capillary Leak / Preeclampsia

Vaginal Bleeding

CRITICAL

Any amount of bright red or dark vaginal bleeding, with or without uterine abdominal pain or back cramping.

⚠️ Clinical Concern: Placental Abruption / Placenta Previa

Decreased Fetal Movement

CRITICAL

Fewer than 10 fetal kicks/rolls in 2 hours while resting on your side, or a distinct cessation of normal activity.

⚠️ Clinical Concern: Acute Fetal Distress / Uteroplacental Insufficiency

Fluid Leaking from Vagina

URGENT

Sudden gush or continuous trickle of watery, clear, or greenish fluid before 37 weeks or labor onset.

⚠️ Clinical Concern: Preterm Premature Rupture of Membranes (PPROM) / Infection
